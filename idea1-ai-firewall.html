SENTINEL PROXY

Scammers call you. An AI answers. You never knew they tried.

01 — What Is It

Every scam requires a human being to be manipulated. Remove the human from first contact — and the attack fails structurally. SentinelProxy is a personal AI agent that interposes itself between you and every inbound unknown communication: calls, messages, emails. It doesn't block them. It engages them fully, behaving exactly like you, while you receive nothing until the interaction is verified safe.

🤖

The AI Proxy

For unknown callers, an AI that sounds and responds like you handles the entire interaction. It's indistinguishable from a real conversation — which means scammers burn time, reveal techniques, and expose themselves to a system that never panics, never complies, and never loses money.

🔐

Verified Circle Pass-Through

Family, friends, and trusted contacts are enrolled. Their calls pass through instantly — invisible to them, instant for you. The protection layer only activates for unknown or unverified contacts. You never experience friction with people who matter.

🕵️

Scammer Intelligence Collection

Every fraudulent contact becomes a data source. The AI extracts the script, the behavioral pattern, the claimed identity — and contributes it anonymously to a shared fraud intelligence network. A new pig-butchering variant used in Brazil is flagged globally within hours.

🧠

Long-Game Containment

For multi-week grooming attacks like romance fraud, the AI can sustain the relationship — keeping the scammer engaged, gathering evidence — while the real person never receives a single manipulative message. The attack is reversed: the scammer becomes the one being studied.
